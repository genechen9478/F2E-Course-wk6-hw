Vegetables

Carrots are healthy vegetables that are usually orange, but they can also be purple, yellow, or white. They are rich in vitamin A, which helps keep our eyes healthy. Carrots have a naturally sweet taste and can be eaten raw, cooked, or added to soups and salads. They are nutritious, delicious, and easy to include in our daily meals.

Corn is a popular crop that is grown in many countries around the world. It is usually yellow and has a sweet taste. Corn is rich in carbohydrates, which provide energy for our bodies. It can be boiled, grilled, or used to make popcorn and other foods. Corn is both delicious and nutritious, making it a common ingredient in many meals.

Peppers are colorful vegetables that come in red, yellow, green, and orange. They have a crunchy texture and a slightly sweet taste. Peppers are rich in vitamins A and C, which help keep our bodies healthy. They can be eaten raw, cooked, or added to salads, stir-fries, and other dishes. Peppers are delicious, nutritious, and easy to use in many recipes.
